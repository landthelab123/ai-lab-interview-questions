# AI lab interview questions (2026)

2330 real engineering interview questions from 52 AI labs and top tech companies — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, SpaceXAI and more — by company and round, with how often each is asked and when it was last reported. Updated every week.

Each question links to its page on **[Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com)**, with a worked answer, a rubric for L4/L5/L6 and an AI coach that grades your own attempt.

## Findings

Frontier labs ask far fewer algorithm puzzles than big tech: 11% of their coding questions against 45% (53% at Google). They ask multi-part builds, simulations and concurrency instead, and 23% of their questions involve LLMs. Full analysis: [What AI labs actually asked engineers in 2026](https://landthelab.com/blog/ai-lab-interview-questions-2026).

## What's here

- **The loop** for each company: its rounds in order, how long each takes and its format.
- **Questions by round**: coding, system design, ML design, practical and behavioral, newest first.
- **Freshness**: how often a question comes up and the month it was last reported. Older than a year reads as possibly retired.

Questions come from candidates' reports and are written up in our own words.
