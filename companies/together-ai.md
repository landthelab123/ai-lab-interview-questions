# Together AI interview questions and loop (2026)

8 engineering interview questions candidates report from Together AI, most recently in Oct 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Together AI on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/together-ai)**.

## The loop

A recruiter call, a 60-minute coding screen, sometimes a take-home for senior or infrastructure roles, then coding on GPU-cluster style problems, an applied ML systems round (attention, streaming, batching, KV cache), system design for senior roles, and a final talk with a leader. Two to four weeks from the first call to an offer is typical.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen | 30 min | Video call |
| 2 | Technical phone screen | 60 min | Live coding in CoderPad |
| 3 | Take-home |  | A realistic production-style task |
| 4 | Coding | 60 min | Algorithms round |
| 5 | Applied ML systems coding | 60 min | Live coding |
| 6 | System design | 60 min | Design discussion |
| 7 | Team and leader conversations |  | Two to four sessions with engineers from different teams, then 30 to 45 minutes with a senior leader |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Together AI interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/together-ai).
