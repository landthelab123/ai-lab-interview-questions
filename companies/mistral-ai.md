# Mistral AI interview questions and loop (2026)

8 engineering interview questions candidates report from Mistral AI, most recently in Sep 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Mistral AI on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/mistral-ai)**.

## The loop

A recruiter or hiring manager call, a technical screen (ML fundamentals for research roles), coding and system design (on CodeSignal in a 2026 report), then a final panel with teammates and leadership; pragmatic, and fluent LLM knowledge is expected. Three to eight weeks from application to an offer is typical; scheduling sets most of the range.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen | 30 min | Call with a talent partner; not technical |
| 2 | LLM knowledge screen | 60 min | Structured technical check with a Mistral engineer |
| 3 | Coding | 60 min | Live coding in Python |
| 4 | System design | 60 min | Architecture discussion |
| 5 | Final panel |  | Three or four conversations with potential teammates and leadership |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Mistral AI interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/mistral-ai).
