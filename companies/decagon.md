# Decagon interview questions and loop (2026)

16 engineering interview questions candidates report from Decagon, most recently in Nov 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Decagon on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/decagon)**.

## The loop

A recruiter call and a practical coding screen, then an onsite of pair coding, agent-system design, a project deep dive and a values round; three to four weeks overall.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen | 30 min | Call |
| 2 | Technical phone screen | 45 min | Live coding in a CoderPad-style editor |
| 3 | Pair coding | 60 min | Build a small working feature with the interviewer as a teammate |
| 4 | System design | 60 min | Design discussion |
| 5 | Project deep dive | 60 min | Discussion of a project you owned |
| 6 | Behavioral / culture |  | Conversation |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Decagon interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/decagon).
