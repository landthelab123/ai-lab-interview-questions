# Cursor interview questions and loop (2026)

14 engineering interview questions candidates report from Cursor, most recently in Oct 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Cursor on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/cursor)**.

## The loop

A recruiter call that checks you really use the product, one to three applied coding screens with no AI beyond autocomplete, then one or two days at the office building a real project with the team, which you present and defend, often with a founder. One to three weeks from the first call to an offer is typical, the onsite project included.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen | 30 min | Call |
| 2 | Technical screens | 60 min | One to three live coding rounds |
| 3 | Onsite project |  | One or two days at the office building a real project with the team, in a slice of Cursor's codebase |
| 4 | Project review and founder conversation |  | Present and defend what you built; often a conversation with a founder |
| 5 | Other questions |  |  |
| 6 | Unconfirmed |  |  |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Cursor interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/cursor).
