## The loop

An HR screen, a timed HackerRank test of two hard problems, a coding screen, then an onsite of one coding and one system design round. Three to five weeks from the first call to an offer is typical.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen |  | HR call; the team is small and everyone reports to the CEO |
| 2 | Online assessment | 30 min | HackerRank, two hard problems |
| 3 | Technical phone screen |  | Coding |
| 4 | Coding |  | Onsite coding |
| 5 | System design |  | Onsite design |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Pika interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/pika).
