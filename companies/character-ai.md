## The loop

A recruiter call, a hiring manager screen on past scaling work, a 60-minute live coding screen, then a virtual onsite of four or five rounds (coding, LLM serving and inference, system design for stateful chat, behavioral), plus a founder or CTO talk for senior hires. Two to five weeks from application to an offer is typical.
