# Applied Intuition interview questions and loop (2026)

30 engineering interview questions candidates report from Applied Intuition, most recently in Nov 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Applied Intuition on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/applied-intuition)**.

## The loop

A recruiter call, a CoderPad coding screen and a call with the hiring manager (in either order), then an onsite of design and coding. Since mid-2026 many onsites pair a system design with a two- to three-hour build of it using Claude, followed by a presentation of the result. Two to four weeks from the first call to an offer is typical.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen |  | Call |
| 2 | Online assessment |  | Timed CoderPad assessment, a few short questions |
| 3 | Technical phone screen | 45 min | Live coding in CoderPad; the language can be fixed by the role (Python, C++, or C for embedded) |
| 4 | Hiring manager |  | Call with the hiring manager or team lead |
| 5 | System design | 45 min | Design discussion, often in Excalidraw or CoderPad's drawing tool |
| 6 | AI-assisted build | 120 min | Build the designed system, or a small web app, in a prepared environment over two to three hours |
| 7 | Project presentation | 30 min | Present what you built in the onsite, or a prepared past project |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Applied Intuition interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/applied-intuition).
