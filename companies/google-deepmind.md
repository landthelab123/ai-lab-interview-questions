# Google DeepMind interview questions and loop (2026)

3 engineering interview questions candidates report from Google DeepMind, most recently in Nov 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Google DeepMind on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/google-deepmind)**.

## The loop

Recruiter and hiring manager screens, one or two coding screens, then a five-to-seven-round loop whose mix depends on the track (software engineer or research engineer), followed by a hiring committee and team match; six to ten weeks is typical.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen | 30 min | Call |
| 2 | Hiring manager |  | Conversation with the hiring manager |
| 3 | Technical phone screen |  | One or two coding sessions in a shared editor |
| 4 | Coding | 60 min | Live coding, usually two rounds; research engineers get ML coding (a loss, attention, a sampling routine or a small training loop) |
| 5 | System design | 60 min | Design discussion |
| 6 | Domain depth or paper presentation | 60 min | Deep technical conversation; research engineers often present and defend a paper |
| 7 | Behavioral | 60 min | Conversation |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Google DeepMind interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/google-deepmind).
