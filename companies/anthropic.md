# Anthropic interview questions and loop (2026)

62 engineering interview questions candidates report from Anthropic, most recently in Oct 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Anthropic on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/anthropic)**.

## The loop

A recruiter call, a leveled CodeSignal assessment and a live practical screen, then about five onsite rounds over two days: coding and system design usually come first, and a weak first day can cancel the second (hiring manager, project deep dive, culture). Two coworker references are checked before the offer. Three to eight weeks from application to an offer is typical; team matching and references take the longest.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen | 30 min | Phone or video call with a recruiter |
| 2 | CodeSignal assessment | 90 min | Timed CodeSignal project in levels; the tests of one level must pass before the next one opens |
| 3 | Live technical screen | 55 min | One engineer on video with screen share, usually in CodeSignal (Google Colab for research roles), Python by default; one practical problem that grows in parts |
| 4 | Hiring manager | 30 min | Call with the hiring manager |
| 5 | Coding | 55 min | Live coding on a realistic, multi-part problem, a different one from the screen |
| 6 | System design | 55 min | Design discussion, often typed into a shared Google Doc rather than drawn; some rounds let you share any drawing tool |
| 7 | Culture and values | 60 min | Conversation, often two interviewers (one asks, one shadows) |
| 8 | Project deep dive | 60 min | You present a project you owned end to end for about 20 minutes; interviewers probe it for the rest of the hour |
| 9 | Unconfirmed |  |  |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Anthropic interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/anthropic).
