# Notion interview questions and loop (2026)

13 engineering interview questions candidates report from Notion, most recently in Jun 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Notion on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/notion)**.

## The loop

A recruiter call, a coding screen (sometimes after a take-home editor project), then an onsite of practical coding and debugging, collaboration-focused system design, behavioral and a leadership conversation. Two to five weeks from the first call to an offer is typical.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen | 30 min | Call |
| 2 | Take-home |  | A small block-based editor in React and TypeScript (block types, drag and drop, undo and redo), about 4 to 8 hours of work |
| 3 | Technical phone screen | 60 min | Live coding in CoderPad |
| 4 | Practical coding and debugging | 60 min | Coding or debugging inside a realistic codebase |
| 5 | System design | 60 min | Design discussion |
| 6 | Behavioral | 60 min | Conversation |
| 7 | Hiring manager / leadership | 45 min | Conversation, sometimes with a founder or early employee |
| 8 | Unconfirmed |  |  |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Notion interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/notion).
