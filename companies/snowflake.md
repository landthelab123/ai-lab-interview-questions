# Snowflake interview questions and loop (2026)

80 engineering interview questions candidates report from Snowflake, most recently in Oct 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Snowflake on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/snowflake)**.

## The loop

A recruiter screen that turns many candidates away, two back-to-back coding screens, then an onsite (virtual or in person) of more coding, system design and a behavioral or hiring-manager round, then a hiring-manager call.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen | 30 min | Call with a recruiter |
| 2 | Two coding screens | 60 min | Two back-to-back live coding rounds, often on the same day; some loops pair coding with system design |
| 3 | Coding | 60 min | Live coding; senior loops may agree a small storage component together, then build it |
| 4 | System design | 60 min | Design discussion |
| 5 | Hiring manager |  | Behavioral conversation |
| 6 | Unconfirmed |  |  |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Snowflake interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/snowflake).
