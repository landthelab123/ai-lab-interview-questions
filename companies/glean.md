# Glean interview questions and loop (2026)

27 engineering interview questions candidates report from Glean, most recently in Oct 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Glean on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/glean)**.

## The loop

Recruiter and hiring manager calls and a fast-paced coding screen, then a virtual onsite of four to five sessions: coding, a two-hour build-from-scratch assignment, enterprise-search system design and behavioral; senior candidates may also meet a founder. Two to five weeks from the first call to an offer is typical.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen | 30 min | Call |
| 2 | Hiring manager | 45 min | Video call |
| 3 | Technical phone screen | 60 min | Live coding, one or two sessions |
| 4 | Coding |  | Live coding, usually two rounds |
| 5 | Practical assignment | 120 min | Build a small working module from an empty file |
| 6 | System design | 60 min | Design discussion |
| 7 | Behavioral | 60 min | Conversation |
| 8 | Unconfirmed |  |  |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Glean interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/glean).
