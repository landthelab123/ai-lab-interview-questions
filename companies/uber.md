# Uber interview questions and loop (2026)

118 engineering interview questions candidates report from Uber, most recently in Oct 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Uber on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/uber)**.

## The loop

A short recruiter call, an online assessment, a 60-minute coding screen, then four 60-minute virtual rounds: two coding (one often object-oriented design), system design (a project deep dive below senior) and a hiring-manager round.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen | 20 min | Call with a recruiter |
| 2 | Online assessment | 90 min | Two problems on a HackerRank-based platform, camera and screen share on |
| 3 | Technical phone screen | 60 min | One medium problem with a follow-up, or two short ones; sometimes object-oriented design |
| 4 | Coding | 60 min | Two rounds; one is often object-oriented design (a parking lot, a meeting scheduler, an expression evaluator) |
| 5 | System design | 60 min | Whiteboard and discussion |
| 6 | Hiring manager |  | Behavioral questions plus a walkthrough of a past project |
| 7 | Unconfirmed |  |  |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Uber interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/uber).
