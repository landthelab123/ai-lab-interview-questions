# SpaceXAI interview questions and loop (2026)

*Formerly xAI.*

40 engineering interview questions candidates report from SpaceXAI, most recently in Oct 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[SpaceXAI on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/xai)**.

## The loop

Fast (often two to three weeks) and almost entirely hands-on coding: a 15-minute screen with an engineer, for some tracks a four-hour take-home, a live applied coding screen, then two or three in-person rounds of coding and design, with ownership probed by the hiring manager rather than in a separate behavioral round.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Short screen with an engineer | 15 min | Video call, often with an engineer rather than a recruiter: a quick intro, your hardest project, why xAI |
| 2 | Take-home | 240 min | Four-hour CodeSignal project; pick one of several prompts (backend service, RAG service, frontend or mobile app), then record a demo video |
| 3 | Technical phone screen | 45 min | Live applied coding on a shared platform such as CoderPad |
| 4 | Onsite applied coding |  | Two or three rounds, in person (Palo Alto for many roles) |
| 5 | System design |  | Scope a vague prompt, then write working code for it |
| 6 | Hiring manager |  | Call or meeting with the hiring manager or a team member |
| 7 | Project presentation |  | Present a large project you owned, then questions |
| 8 | Unconfirmed |  |  |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [SpaceXAI interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/xai).
