# Cohere interview questions and loop (2026)

12 engineering interview questions candidates report from Cohere, most recently in Oct 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Cohere on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/cohere)**.

## The loop

A recruiter call, a hiring manager conversation and production-minded coding rounds, then enterprise-flavored ML and system design, a behavioral round and a team match; four to seven weeks overall.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen | 30 min | Call |
| 2 | Hiring manager |  | Conversation with the hiring manager |
| 3 | Technical phone screen | 60 min | Live coding in Python or Go |
| 4 | Coding | 60 min | Live coding |
| 5 | ML / system design | 60 min | Design discussion |
| 6 | Behavioral | 60 min | Conversation |
| 7 | Team match | 45 min | Conversation with a prospective team |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Cohere interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/cohere).
