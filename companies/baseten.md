# Baseten interview questions and loop (2026)

12 engineering interview questions candidates report from Baseten, most recently in Oct 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Baseten on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/baseten)**.

## The loop

A short recruiter call, a practical CoderPad screen, then a three-to-four-hour loop of deeper coding on concurrency, model-serving system design and a goals conversation that often includes a founder. Three to six weeks from the first call to an offer is typical, about a month most often; replies between rounds can be slow.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen | 30 min | Call |
| 2 | Technical phone screen | 60 min | Live in CoderPad: a few minutes of intro, then 45 to 50 minutes on one practical problem |
| 3 | Deeper coding | 60 min | Live coding, one or two sessions |
| 4 | System design | 60 min | Design discussion |
| 5 | Goals conversation |  | Conversation, often with a founder |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Baseten interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/baseten).
