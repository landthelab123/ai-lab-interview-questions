# Abridge interview questions and loop (2026)

9 engineering interview questions candidates report from Abridge, most recently in Oct 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Abridge on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/abridge)**.

## The loop

A recruiter call, a hiring-manager conversation about a complex past project with a short design discussion, then an onsite panel of system design, coding tied to the product and a cross-functional round, and a final call with the CTO or executives. About a month from the first call to an offer is typical, with a reply about a week after each stage.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen |  | Call with a recruiter |
| 2 | Hiring manager |  | A complex project you built, with a small design discussion |
| 3 | Coding |  | Coding tied to the product, not puzzles; mobile candidates had a one-hour hands-on build |
| 4 | System design |  | General system design |
| 5 | Cross-functional and behavioral |  | Product sense and collaboration, sometimes with a designer |
| 6 | CTO call |  | Final conversation with the CTO or two executives |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Abridge interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/abridge).
