# Meta interview questions and loop (2026)

173 engineering interview questions candidates report from Meta, most recently in Nov 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Meta on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/meta)**.

## The loop

A recruiter call, sometimes a CodeSignal assessment, a 45-minute coding screen, then an onsite that since April 2026 runs AI-enabled: two AI-enabled coding rounds, one or two system design rounds and a behavioral round. Hiring committee and team matching follow.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen |  | Call with a recruiter |
| 2 | CodeSignal assessment | 90 min | Four levels on one class: basic operations, a ranking query, timestamps and expiry, then merge or snapshot and restore |
| 3 | Technical phone screen | 45 min | Two coding problems in CoderPad, about 20 minutes each |
| 4 | AI-enabled coding | 60 min | Several progressive tasks on a starter codebase in CoderPad with an AI assistant panel; usually two rounds |
| 5 | System design | 45 min | Design discussion; either an LLM-agent system or a classic product system |
| 6 | AI/ML design |  | ML system design |
| 7 | Behavioral | 45 min | Behavioral interview |
| 8 | Unconfirmed |  |  |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Meta interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/meta).
