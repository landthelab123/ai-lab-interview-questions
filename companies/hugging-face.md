# Hugging Face interview questions and loop (2026)

10 engineering interview questions candidates report from Hugging Face. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Hugging Face on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/hugging-face)**.

## The loop

A recruiter or hiring manager call, a coding screen with ML or systems depth for some roles, a role-specific take-home on a real open-source problem, a 60-minute review where you defend it, then talks with the team on open-source work and values; no algorithm gauntlet. Three to six weeks from application to an offer is typical; the take-home takes the longest.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen | 30 min | Intro call with a recruiter or the hiring manager; often two or three calls including future teammates |
| 2 | Technical phone screen | 60 min | Live coding |
| 3 | Take-home |  | A task drawn from real work: fix a bug in a real repository, add a feature to a library, a dataset contribution, a Spaces demo; ML roles may get a small training or RLHF task |
| 4 | Take-home review | 60 min | Walk through and defend your solution, maintainer style |
| 5 | Domain depth |  | One or two discussions, depending on the role |
| 6 | Open source and values |  | Conversations with the team, sometimes a lead or executive |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Hugging Face interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/hugging-face).
