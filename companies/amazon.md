# Amazon interview questions and loop (2026)

287 engineering interview questions candidates report from Amazon, most recently in Oct 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Amazon on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/amazon)**.

## The loop

An online assessment for many early-career and SDE II roles, a recruiter call, a 60-minute coding screen with Leadership Principles questions, then four or five 55-minute interviews: coding (often object-oriented design), system design, a hiring-manager round and a Bar Raiser, with behavioral questions in every round.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Online assessment |  | Coding problems plus a repository bug-fix section with a built-in assistant or a design scenario, then a work-style survey |
| 2 | Recruiter screen |  | Call with a recruiter |
| 3 | Technical phone screen | 60 min | Live coding in a shared editor that does not run code, plus Leadership Principles questions |
| 4 | Coding | 55 min | Usually two rounds; one is often an object-oriented design built in steps |
| 5 | System design | 55 min | Design discussion |
| 6 | Hiring manager | 55 min | Behavioral deep dive |
| 7 | Bar Raiser | 55 min | Interview with someone from outside the hiring team |
| 8 | Unconfirmed |  |  |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Amazon interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/amazon).
