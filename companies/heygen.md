## The loop

A recruiter call (sometimes a third-party recruiter), an online assessment or a one-hour coding screen, then a short loop: a coding round, a system design round on some teams, and talks with the hiring manager (sometimes the CTO) and team members. Candidates call it lighter than most; three to four weeks end to end.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen |  | Call, sometimes with an outside recruiter |
| 2 | Online assessment |  | Online assessment, for some roles instead of a live screen |
| 3 | Technical phone screen | 60 min | Live coding |
| 4 | System design |  | One round, on some teams |
| 5 | Hiring manager or CTO | 30 min | Conversation |
| 6 | Team members | 30 min | One or two conversations |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [HeyGen interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/heygen).
