# ElevenLabs interview questions and loop (2026)

9 engineering interview questions candidates report from ElevenLabs, most recently in Nov 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[ElevenLabs on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/elevenlabs)**.

## The loop

A recruiter screen, a 90-minute asynchronous coding test, a product-minded behavioral round, a practical coding round on a dubbing or audio-transcript app, and a final product decomposition discussion. Two to four weeks from application to an offer is typical.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen |  | Call with a recruiter |
| 2 | Online assessment | 90 min | Asynchronous CoderPad test |
| 3 | Culture and values |  | Behavioral round with a product angle |
| 4 | Practical coding |  | Hands-on coding on a small product: the dubbing review workflow, or a React app that plays audio with its transcript |
| 5 | Product decomposition |  | Verbal design discussion |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [ElevenLabs interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/elevenlabs).
