# Tesla interview questions and loop (2026)

38 engineering interview questions candidates report from Tesla, most recently in Sep 2026. Worked answers, a rubric per level and a prep plan: **[Tesla on Land the Lab](https://landthelab.com/companies/tesla)**.

## The loop

Less standard than most: a recruiter call, a 45-minute technical screen that mixes project talk with a compact runnable task, then a second team-run technical round or, for senior roles, an onsite of 45-minute rounds: project deep dive, a manager conversation and system design.

| # | Round | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter screen |  | Call with a recruiter or sourcer |
| 2 | Technical phone screen | 45 min | 10 to 20 minutes on your background, then one compact coding, debugging or design task in CoderPad that must run |
| 3 | Second technical round | 45 min | Coding, often combined with team fit |
| 4 | Project deep dive | 45 min | A project you owned, discussed in depth |
| 5 | Hiring manager | 45 min | Behavioral and manager conversation |
| 6 | System design | 45 min | System or technical design; two rounds in senior loops |
| 7 | Unconfirmed |  |  |

What each round grades, its AI policy and how to prep: [Tesla interview process](https://landthelab.com/companies/tesla).
